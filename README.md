Entry for make.fun's [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) bounty ($2 in USDC).

## The sentence

> The escalator handrail drifts a fraction slower than the steps, so your hand arrives at the top a little behind you.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, no wall clock) writes the
one-sentence entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. The program validates
the sentence against the competition gate first: 1–200 characters, exactly
one sentence, not only whitespace. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
